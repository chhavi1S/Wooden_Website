BioMass Pellets

Biomass pellets are a renewable source of heat energy, produced using agricultural waste such as crop stalks, straw, rice husk, cotton stalk, coffee husk, alfalfa straw, coconut shell, palm shell, and sugarcane bagasse. Solid wastes like junk paper and cardboard are also used as raw materials.

Due to their high density, biomass pellets burn longer and produce consistent heat energy. At Amman Bio Energy Fuels, we utilize specialized equipment at our factory to manufacture high-quality, world-class pellets that serve as an excellent substitute for fossil fuels.

We are a leading supplier and manufacturer of biomass pellets in Tamil Nadu, India. Our pellets are carefully packed in durable containers to maintain their quality over a long period, ensuring optimal performance for industrial use.

Applications of Biomass Pellets:

  • Industrial Boilers
  • Pharmaceutical Industries
  • Food Processing Units
  • Chemical Plants
  • Textile Industries
  • Power Generation Units

Wood Chips

Wood chips are small and medium-sized wood pieces derived from large wood sources. At Amman Bio Fuels, we carefully select high-quality, well-grown woods to maintain optimal thickness, ensuring higher energy output from our wood chips.

We are a leading supplier of wood chips across Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and other parts of India and international markets. Many global clients rely on our wood chips as a major energy source.

The raw materials for wood chips include pulpwood, waste wood, and residual wood from agriculture, landscaping, logging, and sawmills. They are also produced from leftover forestry materials like tree crowns, branches, unsaleable materials, or undersized trees.

Our specialized equipment ensures wood chips with a typical longest dimension ranging from 20-50 mm, offering consistent quality and performance.

Applications of Wood Chips:

  • Gardening
  • Industrial solid heat energy source
  • Biofuel production

Charcoal Briquettes

At Amman Bio Fuels, we manufacture high-quality Charcoal Briquettes using coconut shells — a sustainable green energy product derived from coconut shell waste. Our charcoal briquettes are known for their excellent combustion properties, providing long-lasting and consistent heat energy.

Charcoal briquettes are a cleaner alternative to traditional fuels and are ideal for a wide range of industrial and commercial heating applications. We ensure that each briquette maintains optimal density and moisture content for efficient performance.

Additionally, we offer customized sizing based on client requirements to suit different operational needs, ensuring flexibility and satisfaction.

Applications of Charcoal Briquettes:

  • Industrial boilers and furnaces
  • Food processing industries - Distilleries, Bakeries, Canteens, Restaurants, Dyeing units
  • Fuel for gasifiers to generate electricity
  • Alternative fuel for hospitality and catering sectors
