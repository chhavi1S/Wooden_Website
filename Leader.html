Our Leadership

Visionary leaders driving innovation in agricultural technology.

MR. M ANNADURAI

Managing Director

Annadurai combines business sense with technological prowess. His observations of modern farm machinery in Gulf countries inspired him to adapt these technologies for Indian farmers.

After collaborating with KARTAR Agro Industries, he modified the track combine harvester to produce full-length paddy straw and invented the baler to simplify collection.

He continues to pioneer electric agricultural machinery through our R&D Division.

"Applying modern science to revolutionize agriculture is our mission."

MR. A SANTHOSH KUMAR

Managing Director

Santhosh holds a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering. Since joining in 2015, he has contributed significantly in Quality, Strategic Planning, and Business Excellence.

Under his leadership, our R&D team developed the Multi Crop Chopper and Baler, while expanding our agricultural machinery business globally.

He's a Member of AMMA-India, helping shape agricultural technology's future.

"Engineering solutions that empower farmers is our responsibility."
